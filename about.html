About

A biochemist who learned to code, and never lost the discipline.

I started in a laboratory, running medical biochemistry and microbiology work: biochemical assays, hematological evaluations, microbiological cultures, phlebotomy, and specimen processing, all under strict regulatory SOPs. Documenting every result with care was just part of the job. That training never left me.

I began learning frontend web development in 2025 and completed my training at Digital Fortress Institute in January 2026, bringing the same rigor to building for the web: responsive, production-grade sites in HTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript and React, managed end-to-end from design through deployment. My earliest builds, including the Cheliv Compassionate Care and E-Commerce Showcase sites, were done while I was still in training. Everything since has been built after finishing that program.

Since June 2026 I have worked as a Social Media Manager at First Experts Logistics Solutions, where I also rebuilt the company's website from scratch, and since 2025 I have taken on freelance frontend projects on the side. Before tech, I spent four years in clinical laboratory science and health insurance operations, drawing blood samples, running assays, and reviewing patient claims. That's where I learned to be careful, structured, and outcome-driven with every project. I hold a BSc in Biochemistry from Abia State University.
