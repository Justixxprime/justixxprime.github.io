Fernhollow

A boutique cabin and forest-lodge booking site concept: browse stays, pick dates, and book in three steps, with accounts, gift cards, and a full trip-planning flow.

HTML5 Tailwind CSS JavaScript No backend

The challenge

Booking flows are one of the harder e-commerce patterns to get right client-side: date logic, gift-card balances that actually deduct correctly, account state, and printable confirmations all need to behave consistently across more than twenty pages.

The approach

Built the full booking flow (stay browsing, date search, checkout, gift cards, a local-only demo account system, and printable confirmations) entirely in hand-written JavaScript, then rolled UI fixes out consistently across every page rather than patching individual screens.

Design

A warm, editorial hospitality look (serif typography, a soft header-to-hero blend on every page) with printables that get a real branded letterhead rather than a stripped-down browser printout.

Development

Plain HTML, Tailwind CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Gift card balances genuinely deduct at checkout and persist; contact and gift-code emails send for real via Web3Forms; accounts are an honestly-labeled local-only demo system, not real authentication.

What I solved

  • A gift-card system where balances actually deduct and persist correctly across bookings, not just a one-time redeemed flag.
  • Real email delivery for the contact form and gift-code sending, via Web3Forms.
  • A redesigned mobile menu with multiple ways to close it, and consistent header/hero styling rolled out across all pages.

Result

It's live at justixxprime.github.io/fernhollow-booking-website. No backend, which is exactly why the gift-card math had to be right, there's nowhere for a bug to hide when it's all sitting in the browser.
